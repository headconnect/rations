# Rations 2.4.1

Fixes from review of 2.4.0:

- Switching an account's gauges from Automatic to Custom now starts from
  exactly what the menu bar shows, so a session or weekly limit you had turned
  off no longer reappears.
- A custom gauge whose limit stops being reported shows `–` in that limit's
  place instead of moving the other one up.
- Rate limits or outages at Google while checking Antigravity's sign-in
  client are reported as such, not as a missing Antigravity install.
- A damaged gauge list in the settings can no longer make every account
  disappear; the readable part is kept.
- The global *At 100 %* session countdown can be turned on even with session
  limits hidden, since custom gauges still use it.
