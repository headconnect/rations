# Rations 2.4.0

- New provider: **Google Antigravity**. Add an Antigravity account and sign
  in with Google in the browser; nothing to paste. Rations shows the same
  quotas as `agy /usage`: a 5-hour and a weekly limit for each model group
  (Gemini; Claude and GPT) and model-only limits such as Gemini 4 Argon's.
  The menu bar shows the most-used 5-hour and weekly limit; the popover all
  of them. Its default tag is `Y`. Needs Antigravity or its `agy` CLI
  installed: Rations reads the sign-in client from it rather than shipping it.
- **Custom gauges.** Under Accounts → Gauges, switch an account from
  Automatic to Custom to choose which limits reach the menu bar, add more
  gauges (e.g. `YC` for Antigravity's Claude and GPT limits, or Claude's Fable
  allowance), and put spacers between them: `|` by default, any short text, a
  plain gap, or an image. The first gauge keeps the account's tag or icon;
  the others can have their own.
- **New settings window.** Tabs for Accounts, Menu Bar, General, Usage Hub and
  About. Accounts is a list (drag to reorder) with the selected account's
  settings beside it. Removing an account now asks first. The window keeps one
  size across tabs.
- About shows the version and links to the source, release notes, issues and
  security policy.

Existing accounts and settings are unchanged; accounts stay on Automatic until
you switch them.
